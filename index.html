ABOUT

I'm an IBM z/OS System Programmer with 8+ years supporting enterprise IMS DB/DC environments across the banking and insurance domains. My work centers on subsystem configuration, SMP/E maintenance, performance certification, and observability enablement — including OpenTelemetry and IBM Z Digital Integration Hub initiatives.

I've resolved high-severity performance bottlenecks for one of Spain's largest banks, led modernization efforts, and engineered automation inside highly regulated financial environments. I care deeply about high availability, change discipline, and infrastructure resilience.

Away from the mainframe, I build mobile products — Artha and Settl — because I like shipping things people actually use.
