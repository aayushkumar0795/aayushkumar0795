about

I ship AI-powered products end to end, and I spend my days making some of the most regulated infrastructure on the planet a little smarter.

I build React Native and TypeScript frontends, Python and Node.js services on Supabase/PostgreSQL, and LLM features on the Claude API. I've designed MCP servers that let AI agents work with mainframe subsystems, and built QuantumShield, an AI agent for post-quantum cryptography migration.

At IBM I keep mission-critical IMS environments fast, observable and resilient for enterprise customers, including resolving high-severity performance problems for one of Spain's largest banks. On the side I build products people actually use: Layman Terms, Artha, Mosaic and Settl.
